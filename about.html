About Our Café

Our Story

Savanna Brew Café is a locally owned café located in Westlands, Nairobi, founded by Mrs. Amina Otieno. The café was created from a passion for good coffee, delicious baked snacks and friendly customer service.

We proudly use quality Kenyan coffee beans sourced from Nyeri and Kiambu. Our goal is to give customers a relaxing place where they can enjoy freshly prepared drinks and treats.

Our Mission

Our mission is to serve quality coffee and freshly prepared snacks while providing excellent customer service in a welcoming and comfortable environment.

Our Vision

Our vision is to become a favourite café in Nairobi, recognised for quality products, friendly service and a memorable customer experience.

Our Values

Quality: We value fresh ingredients and carefully prepared products.

Hospitality: We treat every customer with warmth and respect.

Community: We appreciate Kenyan farmers and support locally sourced coffee.
