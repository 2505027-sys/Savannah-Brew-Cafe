About Savanna Brew Café

Savanna Brew Café is a locally owned café located in Westlands, Nairobi, Kenya. Founded by Mrs. Amina Otieno, the café was created to provide a welcoming space where people can enjoy quality coffee and freshly prepared snacks.

We proudly source our coffee beans from Nyeri and Kiambu, supporting the rich coffee culture of Kenya.

Our Story

What started as a passion for coffee has grown into a place where friends, families and professionals can relax, connect and enjoy memorable experiences.

Our Mission

To provide quality Kenyan coffee, delicious freshly baked treats and excellent customer service in a comfortable and welcoming environment.
